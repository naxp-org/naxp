naxp error codes

naxp error reporting is founded on invariant error codes, e.g. NAXP1031. (In contrast, the associated error messages may vary, because they include some of the invalid text or because they are later revised for improved clarity or even translated into another language.)

Error codes NAXP1001 to NAXP1999 are reserved for parsing and other naxp errors. Validation and error reporting are identical across all implementations.

Error codes from NAXP2001 upwards are used for build errors, currently produced only by the C# source generator.
